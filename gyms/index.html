How the pilot works

Four weeks from poster to results.

  1. Week 0

    Setup

    We make a poster with a QR code and a separate code for each location, so we can see where people join most. You put it up at the entrance and in the locker room.

  2. Weeks 1 to 4

    Duels

    Members install the app, add friends and challenge each other. Every week we publish a Duel of the week and tag your gym.

  3. Week 4

    Results and a decision

    We send an overview per location: how many members joined and how many duels were played. Together we choose what to build first and whether to continue.

What we ask of your gym

  • A poster with a QR code at the entrance and in the locker room of each pilot location.
  • One Instagram post or story about the pilot. We prepare the text and the image.
  • Permission to film a short training video, with the consent of members in the frame.
