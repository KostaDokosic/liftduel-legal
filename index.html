Questions, answered

Short answers about LiftDuels.

What is LiftDuels?

LiftDuels is a workout tracker app for the gym. You log sets, reps and weight, follow your progress and challenge friends to strength duels where you both do the same workout and the score updates set by set. It is available on iOS, and an Android version is coming soon.

Is LiftDuels free?

Yes, the app is free to download and use. LiftDuels PRO is optional and adds things like unlimited duels, AI workout plans and all ready made programs. PRO is available as a monthly or yearly subscription, with a free trial on the yearly plan, or as a lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the app before you buy.

How does a strength duel work?

You choose a workout and challenge a friend. When they accept, you both train the same exercises at the same time and log your own sets. Each exercise goes to whoever lifted more, and the duel score changes after every set. At the end you compare results.

Is the scoring fair if my friend is stronger?

Duels compare what each person actually lifted in the same workout, so a stronger friend usually wins: there is no handicap. Assisted exercises are scored by the real load, and the effort you log (reps in reserve) decides when two sets are close. Duels are for fun and points only, there are no prizes.

Can I log workouts without internet?

Yes. Logging a workout works offline. Features that need other people or the server, such as duels, the Wall and AI, need a connection.

Is there an Android app?

Not yet. The Android app is in closed testing and is coming soon. We have no release date to share. The iOS app is on the App Store now.

Does LiftDuels suggest what weight to lift?

Yes. It suggests weight and reps from your own history. Suggestions are only advice: they never change a plan or a workout by themselves. LiftDuels is for training and fitness, not medical advice.

What data do you collect, and can I delete my account?

Analytics start only if you agree. AI features send data to our AI provider only after you agree in the app. You can delete your account and your data inside the app, in Settings, Account. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

Who can use LiftDuels?

LiftDuels is for people aged 16 and over.

Can my gym use LiftDuels?

Yes. We offer gyms a free 30 day pilot. See the gym page, or email support@liftduels.app.
