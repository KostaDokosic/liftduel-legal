LiftDuels support

Contact

Email kosta99dt@gmail.com. We answer within 2 working days. Please include your username and the app version (Settings → About).

Report a person or content

In the app, use Report on a profile, a Wall workout or a duel. You can also Block anyone (Settings → Safety lists blocked people). Reports are answered within 24 hours.

Subscriptions and purchases

Subscriptions are managed by the store: on iPhone, Settings → your name → Subscriptions; on Android, Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. To restore a purchase, open the LiftDuels PRO screen and tap Restore purchases. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google.
