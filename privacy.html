2. Legal bases

Performing our contract with you (providing the app), your consent (optional health-related data such as body weight and sex, push notifications; you can withdraw at any time by removing the data, turning notifications off or deleting your account), and our legitimate interest in security, abuse prevention and improving the app.

3. Who receives data (processors)

  • Supabase – database, authentication, file functions and hosting of your account data.
  • PostHog (EU region) – product analytics.
  • Sentry – crash and error reports, identified by user id only.
  • RevenueCat – subscription and purchase management, together with Apple App Store and Google Play who process the payment.
  • Expo (push service), Apple and Google – delivery of push notifications; Apple and Google also provide sign-in.
  • Anthropic – processes AI requests. Only the data needed for the request is sent, not your email or username. Anthropic does not use API inputs to train its models under its commercial terms.

Some of these providers are outside Serbia and the EU/EEA (for example the United States). Where this happens we rely on their standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards. We may also disclose data when the law requires it.

4. What other users can see

Your username, streak, points, level and duel results are visible to other signed-in users. Your email, body weight and sex are not shown to other users. In a duel your opponent sees your sets and results for that duel.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account in the app (Settings → Account → Delete account) or on our deletion page. Deletion removes your workouts, plans, friends, points, streak, subscription record and push tokens from our database, and asks RevenueCat and PostHog to delete your records. A duel you played stays in your opponent's history with you shown as "Deleted user"; it carries no link to your account or personal data. Crash reports and backups may keep your user id for a limited time and are not purged immediately. Deleting your account does not cancel a store subscription: cancel it in the App Store or Google Play.

6. Your rights

You may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or object to processing, and complain to the supervisory authority (in Serbia the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection; in the EU your local authority). Write to kosta99dt@gmail.com.

7. Children

LiftDuels is for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from younger children; if you believe a child has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Data travels over HTTPS and is stored in Supabase with row-level access rules, so you can read your own data only. No system is perfectly secure.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above changes and, for significant changes, we notify you in the app.
