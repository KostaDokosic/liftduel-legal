Delete your LiftDuel account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open LiftDuel and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account → Delete account.
  3. Confirm twice. Your account and data are deleted immediately.

Without the app

Email kosta99dt@gmail.com from the address you registered with, subject "Delete my LiftDuel account". We delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

Workouts, plans, friends, points, streak, body weight history, subscription record, push tokens, and your analytics records. A duel you played stays in your opponent's history as "Deleted user" with no link to you. Crash reports and backups may keep your anonymous user id for a limited time. A store subscription is not cancelled automatically: cancel it in the App Store or Google Play. See the Privacy Policy.
